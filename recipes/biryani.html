Biryani

Description

Biryani is a celebrated, fragrant South Asian mixed rice dish. A compulsory dish for events in Pakistan as well as in India. It layers fluffy, spiced basmati rice with heavily marinated meat (like chicken, beef, or lamb) or vegetables. Cooked with aromatic spices, saffron, ghee, and fresh herbs, it is slow-steamed in a sealed pot to allow the rich flavors to deeply infuse.

Ingrediants

For the Chicken:\(\frac{1}{2} \text{ cup}\) plain yogurt (curd)\(1\frac{1}{2} \text{ tsp}\) biryani masala powder1 tsp chili powder, \(\frac{1}{2} \text{ tsp}\) turmericSalt, to tasteFor the Rice & Layering:2 cups aged Basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes)Whole spices (1 bay leaf, 4 green cardamoms, 4 cloves, 1-inch cinnamon stick)\(\frac{1}{3} \text{ cup}\) vegetable oil or ghee2 large onions, thinly sliced\(\frac{1}{2} \text{ cup}\) fresh mint leaves & \(\frac{1}{2} \text{ cup}\) fresh cilantroPinch of saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk

For curry

  • 0.25 cup oil
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 lb (approx. 500 g) bone-in chicken cut into medium pieces
  • half cup plain yogurt
  • salt to taste
  • 1 pack shan bombay biryani masala
  • 1 to 2 large lemon thinly sliced
  • 0.25 cup dried alo bukhara (plum)
  • 3 medium tomatoes diced
  • 1 cup chopped cilantro
  • half cup chopped mint

For Rice

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 6 cups water
  • half tsp cumin
  • one or two bay leaves
  • 1 tsp oil
  • salt to taste

Steps

  1. Wash and presoak rice for 2 hours
  2. In a pan put water, cumin, bay leaves, oil and salt. Switch on the stove and put the pan on it.
  3. Add soaked rice (after draining the water) to the boiling water and cook untill 75% done. Then use a colander to drain the water and keep the rice aside
  4. Now in a separate pan, add oil and onion and fry it untill light golden. add ginger garlic paste and fry untill everything becomes golden
  5. Now add chicken and fry it untill it changes color to white.
  6. Add tomatoes and fry until it becomes soft. Then add Biryani masala, salt and mix.
  7. Cook untill oil separates.
  8. Add yogurt and cook on low flame untill oil separates.
  9. The chicken should be tender by now. If not add a little water and cook for 5 more minutes on low flame.
  10. Now the curry is ready.
  11. In another pan put a layer of cooked rice then a layer of gravy and sprinkle cilantro and mint and lemon slices. Then repeat the process untill finish.
  12. Finally, set the stove on medium heat and cover the lid of the pan with damp kitchen cloth. Put the lid on the pan and put weigth on it.
  13. After 5 minutes, set the heat on low and let the rice steam for almost 20 minutes.
  14. After 20 minutes turn of the stove and serve with raita and salad.
